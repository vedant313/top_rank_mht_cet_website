How it works

Student Dashboard, explained.

Here is what you can find in this part of the Top Rank student experience.

01

Your daily revision goal

Use the Daily Revision Goal area as a reminder of the study target you want to work towards each day. It gives your preparation a clear daily focus.

02

Build a study streak

The Study Streak area gives you a quick view of your consistency over time and encourages a steady study routine.

03

Keep key tools close

The dashboard includes shortcuts and sections such as Study Timetable, Practice Tests and Performance, along with course and update areas.

04

Course and preparation updates

The visible dashboard includes the Target MHT-CET 2026 Batch course card, Updates and Strategy sections.
