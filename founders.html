FOUNDER · TOP RANK MHT CET

Jayesh Kumavant

The idea behind Top Rank — and the person who built the app.

Jayesh came up with the idea for Top Rank MHT CET and took the lead in turning that idea into an app. He is the creator and builder of the product, shaping its direction around the needs of students preparing for the Maharashtra Common Entrance Test.

His role centres on the product itself: developing the app, bringing the concept to life and driving the platform forward.

Vedant Bhavsar

04 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

A student-first direction.

Top Rank is focused on making preparation feel more structured and helping students stay connected to their progress.

Our mission

To provide MHT CET aspirants with a useful digital companion that supports organised study, practice and progress tracking.

Our vision

To keep improving the Top Rank experience and make purposeful digital learning tools more accessible to students.

Our approach

Build around student needs, keep improving the product and work together to move the platform forward.
